# Nigeria 2026 payroll review and approval form

**Approval status: pending. No accountant signature or professional approval has been supplied.**

This form accompanies the implemented payroll review workbook and independent engineering reference cases. HR owns staff and salary inputs; a qualified accountant or payroll specialist reviews tax/contribution treatment and representative calculations. One person can cover both roles if suitably qualified. The application records evidence; it does not verify professional credentials or manufacture approval.

## Review packet

- [Payroll scope and limitations](HR-SUITE.md), including PAYE bands, minimum-wage treatment, pension policy, NHF basis, NHIS, rent/other reliefs, ECS/ITF applicability, proration, bonus treatment and cumulative/override methods.
- [Editable review workbook](../public/payroll-review.xlsx).
- [Fixed-month reference cases](../public/payroll-reference-cases.json), [period reference cases](../public/payroll-period-cases.json), and [cumulative reference cases](../public/payroll-cumulative-cases.json). Check the files present in the reviewed revision; do not substitute expected values simply to make differences pass.
- Actual company salary/benefit policies, applicability decisions, relief evidence, prior-year-to-date/opening source balances, and representative staff examples. These must be provided privately by the company and are not included in this repository.
- Released-run calculation fingerprints and the app's recorded external-review evidence, if applicable. A recorded reviewer name alone does not establish accountant approval.
